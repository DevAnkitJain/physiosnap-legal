PhysioSnap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

PhysioSnap is a physiotherapy study app. This policy explains what we collect, why, who it is shared with, and how to get it deleted. It covers the PhysioSnap Android app and the service behind it.

In short: we collect the minimum needed to sign you in, remember what you have learned, and — only if you ask for it — remind you to study. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising profiles. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

1. Who we are

PhysioSnap is operated by the developer identified on the app's Google Play listing. Contact details are in section 11.

2. What we collect

Account information

You sign in with Google. Google gives us your email address, name, profile picture URL, and a Google account identifier. We never see or receive your Google password.

Information you give us

  • Your role (student, intern, practising physiotherapist)
  • Your programme and year of study, if you provide them
  • The clinical areas you are interested in, used to personalise which cards you see
  • Your daily reminder preference and the time you choose

Learning activity

  • Which cards you have read and when, so the app never shows you the same card twice
  • Your daily streak and totals
  • Quiz attempts, answers and scores
  • Cards you bookmark

Device and technical information

  • A push notification token and your time zone, collected only if you turn on daily reminders. The time zone is used so a reminder set for 7pm arrives at 7pm where you are.
  • Standard server logs, including IP address, generated when the app talks to our service. These are used for security and to keep the service running.

Purchases

If you subscribe, payment is handled by Google Play and subscription status is managed through RevenueCat. We receive whether your subscription is active and when it renews or expires. We never receive your card or bank details.

What we do not collect

  • No patient data. Please do not enter any information about real patients into this app.
  • No location data, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party advertising SDK is present in the app.
  • No health data about you.

4. Legal basis

Where the UK/EU GDPR applies, we rely on performance of a contract for the parts needed to run your account and deliver the service, consent for push notifications, which you can withdraw at any time, and our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and improving the content. Where India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act applies, we process your data for the purpose you provided it for.

6. Where your data is held

Our servers and the third-party services above may store and process data outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and learning data: for as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted accounts: personal details are removed or anonymised promptly when you delete your account, and backups age out within 30 days.
  • Server logs: a short rolling window, kept for security and troubleshooting.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account. Profile → Delete Account, inside the app. This removes your account and your learning data, and cannot be undone.
  • Change your details. Profile → Edit Profile and Interests.
  • Turn reminders off. Profile → Daily reminder, or in your device's notification settings. Turning them off removes the push token for that device.
  • Access, correct, or export your data, and object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

PhysioSnap is intended for physiotherapy students and professionals and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Sign-in tokens are held in your device's secure storage. Access to production systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data we hold.

12. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for significant changes we will give notice in the app. Continuing to use PhysioSnap after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Not medical advice

PhysioSnap is an educational study aid. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a substitute for clinical judgement, local protocols, or qualified supervision.
