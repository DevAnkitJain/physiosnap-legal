Delete your PhysioSnap account

Last updated: 1 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your PhysioSnap account and what happens to your data. You can do it yourself inside the app, or ask us to do it for you.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. Your streak, progress and saved cards are lost, and signing in again creates a brand new account.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open PhysioSnap and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm on the dialog that appears.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out. No waiting period, and no need to contact us.

Or ask us to delete it

If you cannot access the app — for example you have lost access to the Google account you signed up with — email us from the address associated with your account:

guidancemitra@gmail.com

Use the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm within 30 days, and usually much sooner. We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before deleting it, so that nobody else can delete it on your behalf.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Email addressDeleted immediately
Name and profile pictureDeleted immediately
Google account identifierDeleted immediately
Your role, programme, year of study and interestsDeleted immediately
Notification token and reminder settingsDeleted immediately — reminders stop at once
Learning history — cards read, streaks, quiz scores, bookmarks Kept in anonymised form. Once your email, name and Google identifier are removed there is nothing connecting these records to you. We keep them only to understand which content helps people learn.
Subscription and payment records Held by Google Play, not by us. We never receive your payment details. Manage or cancel a subscription in the Play Store; deleting your PhysioSnap account does not cancel it.
BackupsDeleted data ages out of routine backups within 30 days

Cancel your subscription first

If you have a Premium subscription, cancel it before deleting your account. Subscriptions are billed by Google Play and deleting your PhysioSnap account does not stop the billing. To cancel: Play Store → your profile picture → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → PhysioSnap → Cancel.
